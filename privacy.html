Hoppibop Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Overview

Hoppibop is an arcade hopping game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles, what remains on your device, and when Apple or Google services may process information.

Hoppibop does not operate its own user-account system or game-data server. Most gameplay information is stored locally on your device. Some features use Apple services, and optional rewarded advertising uses Google services as described below.

Information Hoppibop stores locally

Hoppibop stores gameplay and app-state information on your device so the game can save progress and provide its features. This may include:

  • scores, levels, run history, milestones, and leaderboard-ready score totals;
  • unlocked and selected characters, cosmetics, worlds, mastery progress, favorites, and onboarding progress;
  • virtual-currency balances, rewards, login-reward progress, and summon history;
  • settings such as sound, haptics, motion, and other gameplay preferences;
  • purchase entitlements and local records used to prevent duplicate grants; and
  • the local calendar day and successful-reward count used to enforce the rewarded-ad daily limit.

This information is stored using iOS app storage, including app-support files and preferences. Hoppibop does not transmit the full local save file to a Hoppibop-operated server.

Game Center

Hoppibop supports Apple Game Center. If you use Game Center, Apple authenticates your Game Center account and may provide the app with your Game Player ID, display name, and alias. Hoppibop may submit gameplay score or progress snapshots to Apple leaderboards and may retrieve leaderboard entries, ranks, and player-facing profile information.

Hoppibop does not store your Game Center credentials. Apple controls Game Center accounts and its processing of Game Center information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Hoppibop uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases, including permanent entitlements such as Remove Ads and the Happy Supporter Pack, and consumable virtual-currency packs. Apple processes the purchase and payment information. Hoppibop receives information needed to deliver and restore purchases, such as product identifiers, verified transaction identifiers, entitlement status, and revocation status.

Some purchase and entitlement records are retained locally to deliver purchases reliably and avoid duplicate grants. Hoppibop does not receive your full payment-card details from Apple.

Optional rewarded advertising

Hoppibop includes an optional rewarded-ad feature. You may choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive 5 Fortune. The app allows no more than two successful rewarded-ad grants per local calendar day. The app stores the applicable day and successful-grant count locally.

The Fortune feature does not require a forced interstitial ad. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent ordinary gameplay.

Google Mobile Ads and privacy choices

Optional rewarded ads are provided through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Hoppibop also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent or privacy choices where applicable. Hoppibop gates Google ad requests using UMP's canRequestAds status. When UMP indicates that privacy options are required, the app provides a Privacy Choices control in Settings.

Depending on your region, device settings, Google configuration, and privacy choices, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as IP address, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostic information, consent signals, and information used to deliver, limit, measure, or prevent fraud involving ads. Hoppibop configures Google's maximum ad content rating as G. That setting limits ad content; it does not mean Google receives no information, that ads are anonymous, or that the app is classified as child-directed.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising and data information.

Tracking and advertising identifiers

Hoppibop does not currently present Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and the app's own code does not deliberately add behavioral tracking or directly request access to the advertising identifier (IDFA). Hoppibop does not make the broader claim that third-party services collect no information. Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process information under Google's policies, applicable law, platform restrictions, SDK configuration, and your available consent or privacy choices.

Other data and third-party services

The current app code does not include a production third-party analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not create Hoppibop accounts, accept user-generated content, or request access to contacts, precise location, the camera, microphone, photo library, or push notifications.

The principal third-party services currently integrated are Apple Game Center, Apple StoreKit, Google Mobile Ads, and Google UMP. Apple and Google process information under their own policies and act independently for portions of their services.

Children's privacy and audience

Hoppibop may be enjoyed by a broad audience, but this policy does not describe the app as specifically directed to children. Audience classifications, parental controls, and advertising treatment are handled through applicable platform and provider settings. The G maximum ad content rating is an ad-content setting and is not an age classification or legal certification.

Hoppibop does not knowingly ask users to submit names, email addresses, messages, photos, or other user-generated content to a Hoppibop-operated service. Parents or guardians with a privacy concern may use the contact information below.

Data retention

Local gameplay and settings data generally remain on the device until you reset the relevant data, delete the app, or iOS removes the data. Some purchase records may be retained locally for reliable fulfillment and may also remain in Apple's transaction systems. Game Center, StoreKit, Google Mobile Ads, and UMP information is retained by Apple or Google according to their respective policies and legal obligations.

Data security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect local save and purchase-delivery information, including iOS app storage and verified StoreKit transactions. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your privacy choices

  • You can choose not to watch optional rewarded ads.
  • Where required, use Settings > Privacy Choices in Hoppibop to review choices presented by Google UMP.
  • You can manage Game Center through your Apple and device settings.
  • You can use the app's purchase-restoration features and manage purchases through Apple.
  • You can reset available in-app data or delete the app to remove its local app data, subject to iOS behavior and records retained independently by Apple or Google.

International users

Apple and Google may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Their services apply their own regional terms, safeguards, consent flows, and privacy practices. Review their linked policies for more information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Hoppibop's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy is published.
